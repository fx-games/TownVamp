# 13 — Body discovery and the Hunter

**What to build:** leaving a body where it will be found stops being bookkeeping and
starts being dangerous. A corpse in anyone's line of sight promotes the victim from Absent
to Confirmed Dead, and a Confirmed death puts a Hunter on the streets at night.

Discovery is purely spatial. No dice, no detection rolls — a Townsperson's line of sight
crosses the body and that is that. **Test per corpse rather than per observer**: corpses
are few and Townspeople are many, so the cost stays bounded by the number of bodies lying
around. This is also why no general NPC vision is needed.

A body in the square is found within the hour. A body in a cellar may hold for weeks.

A Hunter walks the town at night once any death is Confirmed, and is the single exception
to the flee-on-sight rule everywhere else in the game: a Hunter pursues. Catching the
vampire ends the run.

**There is no combat.** No health, no damage, no armour, no attack rolls. A Hunter is a
chase and a proximity rule, nothing more — the combat systems were deleted in ticket 02
and none of them come back for this.

Hunters carry light, which puts them under the same occlusion rule that blinds any lit
viewer to unlit tiles. They hunt the vampire with the one thing that prevents them seeing
him. Patrols can use the map format's existing named-path support.
